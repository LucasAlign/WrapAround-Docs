# RSVP to a training event

**Who this is for:** Volunteers and advocates, and anyone who wants to volunteer and
doesn't have an account yet.
**When to use it:** You want to save a spot at an upcoming training date.
**Before you start:** Nothing. If you don't have an account, you create one while you RSVP.

## Steps

1. Open the training events page:
    - **Signed in:** go to **My Training** and find **Scheduled training events**, then
      select an event, or **See all training events**.
    - **Not signed in:** open the events link your organization shared (it ends in
      `/events`).
2. On **Upcoming training**, select the event you want. Each one shows its date, whether
   it's **In person** (with its city) or **Online**, and whether it's **Open** or **Full**.
3. RSVP:
    - **Signed in as a volunteer or advocate:** select **RSVP**. You'll see **You're
      going!**
    - **No account yet:** the event page shows **Create your volunteer account to RSVP**.
      Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Password**, check
      that you agree to the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**, and select
      **Create account and RSVP**. Enter the 6-digit **Code** we email you and select
      **Confirm and RSVP**.
    - **Already have an account but signed out:** select **I already have an account**,
      enter your **Email** and **Password**, then select **Sign in and RSVP**.

## What you'll see

**You're going!** and a confirmation email with the details. Use **Add to calendar** to
save the date. For an in-person event the address arrives by email 48 hours before; for
an online event the meeting link arrives 15 minutes before. Neither is shown on the
public page.

Once you're signed in, your RSVPs also appear under **Your upcoming training events** on
your dashboard.

!!! note "Can't RSVP?"
    A date can't take RSVPs once it's **Full**, its **RSVP deadline** has passed, or it's
    been cancelled. Select **See other training dates** to find another one. RSVPs are for
    volunteers and advocates, so staff and family accounts see **RSVPs are for
    volunteers.**

## Cancel your RSVP

Open the event (or find it under **Your upcoming training events**), select **Cancel
RSVP**, then confirm. Your spot opens up for someone else. You can cancel any time until
the event starts.
