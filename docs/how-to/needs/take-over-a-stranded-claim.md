# Take over a claim from a paused volunteer

**Who this is for:** Volunteers on the family's care team (advocates and program staff can do this too).
**When to use it:** When a need or a day of a recurring need is held by a volunteer who has been paused or disabled, so nobody is coming.
**Before you start:** You're [signed in](../account/accept-invite.md), you're on the family's care team, and your own volunteer account is active.

## Steps

1. From the main menu, open **Schedule**.
2. Find the date marked **volunteer paused** (in the day list) or **Volunteer paused — needs cover** (in a recurring need's date list). These dates are highlighted in red.
3. Tap **Take over this day** on a recurring need, or **Take over** on a one-time need.
4. In the **Take over this day?** confirmation, read the details and tap **Take over this day**. Tap **Never mind** (or **Not yet** in the recurring-need dialog) to back out.
5. A message confirms **You've taken over this day**.

## What you'll see

- You're listed as the volunteer for that date, replacing the paused volunteer.
- On a recurring need, only that one date moves to you. Any other dates the paused volunteer holds stay theirs, so take over each date you want.

!!! note "Why these dates need cover"
    A claim held by a paused or disabled volunteer is still listed, but it no longer counts as coverage, so the need can read as **Open** again. That volunteer has lost access to the family and gets no reminders. Advocates see a count of these needs on the Schedule page and the dashboard (**Needs claimed by paused volunteers**).
