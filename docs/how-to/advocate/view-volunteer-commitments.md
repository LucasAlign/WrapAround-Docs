# See what each volunteer has committed to

**Who this is for:** Advocates and program staff
**When to use it:** To see, for one family, which needs each care-team volunteer has signed up for over the next few weeks, including recurring dates.
**Before you start:** Open the **Needs** page for the family you want to look at.

## Steps

1. Open **Needs** from the navigation.
2. Above the board, click **Volunteer commitments**. (**Posted needs** switches back to the normal board.)
3. To narrow the list, type a name in **Search volunteers…**.
4. To change the window, use the **Date range** menu and choose **Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days** or **Next 90 days**.
5. Click any need under a volunteer to open its details.

## What you'll see

A two-column list with **Volunteer** and **Needs being met**. Each need shows its title, date and time, and, for a recurring need, a plain-language description of the repeat. The line under the filters shows the date range and "Recurring dates included". A volunteer with nothing coming up shows "No upcoming commitments".

Dates a volunteer was excused from, and dates a cover volunteer took over, are already accounted for. Cancelled and closed needs are left out. A volunteer who has left the care team but still holds a commitment stays in the list.

!!! note
    The details window opened from this view is read-only. Use **Posted needs** to edit or reassign a need.
