# View volunteer details

**Who this is for:** Advocate
**When to use it:** To check a volunteer's contact details, training progress, childcare clearance, or which family/families they're assigned to.
**Before you start:** Nothing — the **Volunteers** page lists the volunteers you can see.

## Steps

1. Go to **Volunteers** (under **People** in the menu).
2. Click a volunteer's row. Their profile opens in a panel on the side of the page.

    ![Volunteers & Advocates list with a volunteer's row highlighted.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/volunteers-list.png)

    ![The Volunteers & Advocates list on a phone.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/volunteers-list-mobile.png)

3. Read the badges under their name: their volunteer status, **Trained** or **Untrained**, whether they've signed the volunteer agreement, and their childcare clearance if they have one.
4. Use the tabs to see more:
    - **Overview** — email, phone, address, county, **Childcare Status**, **Partnered Church**, **Attending Church**, and **Serving With** (other churches whose families they serve).
    - **Training** — **Training Progress** (the percentage of required modules complete), childcare clearance, and **Training Modules**, with each module's status.
    - **Family** — the family or families they're assigned to.
    - **History** — sample data only for now. Contact history isn't tracked yet.
    - **Notes** — free-text notes about this volunteer. See [Add notes to a volunteer's profile](add-notes-to-a-volunteer.md).

    ![Volunteer profile panel open on the Overview tab, with status badges under the name.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/profile-overview.png)

    ![The volunteer profile on a phone, Overview tab.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/profile-overview-mobile.png)

    ![Volunteer profile panel on the Training tab, showing Training Progress, childcare clearance and each module's status.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/profile-training.png)

    ![The Training tab on a phone.](../../assets/screens/view-volunteer-details/profile-training-mobile.png)

## What you'll see

On the **Training** tab, each module shows as **Completed**, **In Progress**, or **Not Started**. **Trained** means every required module is complete.

Childcare clearance shows as **Childcare approved**, **Childcare expiring soon**, **Childcare clearance expired**, or **Childcare approval pending**. A volunteer whose clearance has expired can't claim or be assigned childcare needs.
