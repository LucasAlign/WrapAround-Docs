# Update your church's details

**Who this is for:** Advocates.
**When to use it:** Your church's website, contacts or address are out of date.
**Before you start:** A coordinator has assigned you to a church. If not, **My Church**
shows **No church assigned** and nothing is editable.

## Steps

1. In the side menu, open **My Church**.
   It's listed under the secondary links, below the main sections. The page opens ready
   to edit.
2. Review the page. **Church Name**, **Partnership** status and **County** are shown as
   text and can't be changed here; ask a coordinator or admin if one is wrong.
3. Edit the fields you can change:
    - **Website** — for example `gracechurch.org`.
    - **Contacts** — change a contact's **Name**, **Title**, **Phone** or **Email**, click
      **Add contact** to add another, or click **Make primary** on the person families
      should reach first.
    - **Address** — **Street**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**.
4. Click **Save Changes** (or **Save** at the top of the page).

## What you'll see

A **Church updated** message appears and your changes show on the page. If nothing
changed you'll see **No changes to save** instead.

!!! note "What you can't do here"
    You can't rename the church, change its partnership status or county, or delete it.
    Those stay with program staff.
