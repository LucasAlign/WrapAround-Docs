# Sign up for a slot

**Who this is for:** Volunteers and advocates. (Admins and coordinators manage needs but don't
see a sign-up button.)
**When to use it:** When there's an open need or a recurring slot you can cover.
**Before you start:** You're assigned to the family. You can also
[view the calendar](view-calendar.md) to see what's already covered.

## Steps

1. Open **Ways to Help** (volunteers) or **Needs** (leads and advocates) in the sidebar. The
   page is titled **Ways to Help**, and needs nobody has signed up for yet appear under the
   **Available** tab.

    ![The Ways to Help page on the Available tab, with the Volunteer for this button highlighted on the first need.](../../assets/screens/sign-up-slot/needs-board-available.png)

2. Read the details — **what's needed**, **when**, and any notes.
3. Make sure you can commit to it.
4. Click **Volunteer for this**.
    - For a **one-time** need, a **Volunteer for this?** confirmation opens. Click
      **Yes, sign me up**.
    - For a **recurring** need, a picker opens. Choose **I can help** next to a single date
      (then **Confirm commitment**), or choose **I can help with every date** and confirm
      **Claim series** to take the whole series.

    ![A recurring need's picker listing individual dates, each with an I can help button, and an I can help with every date button below.](../../assets/screens/sign-up-slot/pick-a-date.png)

5. The need (or the date you picked) now shows **your name**.

## What you'll see

- The need shows you as the volunteer, and the rest of the team can see it's covered.
- It appears on the [Schedule](view-calendar.md) so you can find it again.

!!! tip "If your plans change"
    If you claimed a slot but can no longer make it, use
    [**Request coverage**](../needs/request-coverage.md) — it asks the care community for
    backup without giving up your spot right away.
