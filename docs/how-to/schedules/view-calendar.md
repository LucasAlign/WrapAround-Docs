# View the calendar

**Who this is for:** Volunteers, advocates, and program staff.
**When to use it:** To see what's coming up for your family and when help is needed.
**Before you start:** You've [accepted your invite and signed in](../account/accept-invite.md),
and you're assigned to a family.

![The Schedule: a month calendar with a legend for assigned/unassigned tasks.](img/volunteer-calendar-desktop.png)

## Steps

1. From the main menu, open **Schedule**.
   On a wide screen you'll see your family's calendar as a **month grid**. A legend shows
   which entries are **Assigned** and which are **Unassigned**, and an icon key shows the
   task types (Meals, Childcare, Transportation, and others).
2. Each day shows its scheduled **events** and **commitments** (needs someone's claimed) —
   tap a day to see its full details.
3. Use the **‹ ›** arrows next to the month name to move between months.
4. On a phone, the Schedule opens as an **Upcoming** list (the agenda) grouped into
   **Today**, **This week** and **Later**. Use the **Agenda** / **Month** toggle at the top
   right to switch views. On a wide screen the Schedule always shows the month grid.

   ![Phone view of the Schedule in Agenda mode: an Upcoming list grouped under Today and Later, with the Agenda and Month toggle at the top right.](../../assets/screens/view-calendar/agenda-view.png)

   Tap **Month** to switch to the calendar grid. The toggle then sits beside the month name.

   ![Phone view of the Schedule in Month mode: a month grid with Assigned and Unassigned legend, and the Agenda and Month toggle beside the month name.](../../assets/screens/view-calendar/month-view.png)

## What you'll see

Your family's upcoming events and your own commitments. From a day's details you can
also act on an entry — for example **Claim** it (a repeating entry offers **Claim this day**
and **Claim series**), or **Give up** a day you're signed up for (see
[Sign up for a slot](sign-up-slot.md)). You can also [claim a need](../needs/browse-needs.md)
on the Needs board.

!!! tip "Recurring events"
    Some entries repeat — a weekly ride, for example. See
    [Understand recurring events](recurring-events.md) so you know what you're committing to.
